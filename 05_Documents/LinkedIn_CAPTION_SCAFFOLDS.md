# LinkedIn caption scaffolds

These are structures, not finished captions. The reflections have to be his: I have not written opinions in his name. Fill each bracket from his own words, or send me a voice note and I will shape it.

Rules used: lead with the idea, not the publication; name the publication once; one line on why it matters now; no hashtags unless the post needs search help; no "excited to share".

## Re-read pilot (LI_REREAD_PILOT_M101_M006)

Structure for every Re-read: old headline captured, the one line that still stands, then three short answers from him.
- What I would keep: [ ]
- What I would change: [ ]
- What I got wrong or underestimated: [ ] (only run the series if he is willing to answer this one)
Pilot pair: Mint 2017 "Secrets to successful succession" and CXO Digitalpulse Dec 2025 "What Quietly Changed Inside India's Family Businesses in 2025".
Open question for him: in 2017 the advice was to plan early; in 2025 families plan while the founder is still in charge. Did the advice hold, or did families get ahead of it?
