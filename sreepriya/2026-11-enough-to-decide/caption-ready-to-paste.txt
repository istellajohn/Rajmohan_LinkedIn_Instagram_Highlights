We will rarely have 100% information or 100% certainty. Leadership still asks us to decide.

There is real wisdom in thinking deeply: reading, researching, questioning assumptions and considering different possibilities. Good leadership should never confuse speed with judgement. But there comes a point where more analysis stops creating clarity and starts creating paralysis.

Some of us look for more certainty before we move. Others are comfortable with calculated risks, learning from outcomes and course-correcting along the way. Neither is wrong. The strength is in the balance: enough deliberation to be responsible, and enough decisiveness to not get stuck.

What I keep coming back to is the discipline in between: thinking deeply, deciding thoughtfully, acting courageously, learning quickly and adapting continuously.

"Far better it is to dare mighty things... even though checkered by failure..." Theodore Roosevelt

When a decision really matters, which way do you lean: more certainty first, or action first?
