## Alt text
1. A boarding pass reading Abroad to Home, departs when the plan is ready. Text: Moving back to India? Check these before you book the flight.
2. A Business Insider headline: I built a tech career in the US, became a citizen, got married, and had kids. We'll finish raising them in India.
3. A timeline from Decision to Landing, with the gap between them marked "this part".
4. A checklist to tick: RSUs or ESOPs abroad, concentrated stock, retirement accounts abroad, property or accounts abroad, school timing, foreign citizenship.
5. Quiz: when should you start unwinding stock built up abroad? A after you land, B before you move, C it depends on your tax status when you sell.
6. Answer C: it depends on your status when you sell. When you sell deserves a plan of its own, made before the move.
7. Three stages: NRI, RNOR and resident. Each stage changes what gets taxed, where, and how long there is to plan around it.
8. True or false: India's new Income-tax Act, 2025 changed the RNOR rules.
9. Answer B, false. In the Income Tax Department's words, you remain not ordinarily resident if you were non-resident in 9 of the 10 preceding years, or in India 729 days or less in the preceding seven.
10. Two orders of planning: A books the flight first; B maps status windows and plans investments before booking. Comment A or B.
11. Cross-border wealth planning, investment restructuring, tax considerations and estate planning brought together in one conversation with Entrust.
12. If coming home is in your next few years, think it through long before the flight is booked. Save, send, read Ghar Wapasi 2.0.
