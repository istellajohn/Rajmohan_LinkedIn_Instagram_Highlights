Quick question · 01
You hold stock built up over years abroad. When should you start unwinding it?
AAfter you land in India
BBefore you move, to be safe

Answer
C
It depends on your status when you sell.
RSUs, ESOPs and concentrated stock built up over years abroad need a plan for when and how to unwind that concentration, without taking an unnecessary hit.
Why the timing matters
The same sale can be taxed differently depending on which status window you are in.
General information, not tax advice.
Sreepriya N S07 / 12
Three status windows
Each one changes what gets taxed, and where.
NRI
Non-resident
Broadly, only income earned or received in India is taxed in India.
RNOR
Resident but not ordinarily resident
The transition window. Income earned and received outside India is generally not taxed in India, with some exceptions.
ROR
Resident
Your worldwide income is taxed in India.
General information under India's Income-tax Act, 2025. Your adviser should confirm your status each year.
