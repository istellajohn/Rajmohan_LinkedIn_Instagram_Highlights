## Caption

Before Xoxoday reached 65 million users, its founders shut down the business they started with.

This Diwali, I met Kushal Agrawal, co-founder of Xoxoday, at a gathering in Raipur hosted by my friend Abhinav Khandelwal of Credalis.

Xoxoday began in 2012 as Giftxoxo, a gifting business, and that year's Diwali season became an important period for its corporate gifting work. Over the next few years the team explored experiences and activities, but the business wasn't scaling the way they had expected. In 2018, the founders shut it down and concentrated on an opportunity they had already seen gaining traction: technology for corporate rewards and incentives. Today, Xoxoday reports serving more than 5,000 businesses and reaching over 65 million end users.

The numbers are impressive. What stayed with me was the decision.

Kushal spoke candidly about sleepless nights, self-doubt and changing direction more than once, sometimes to find a way forward and sometimes simply to survive. A pivot is never only a product decision. Resources are already committed, people are working towards the old plan, and everyone has expectations about where the company is headed. Someone has to recognise what customers are actually responding to, decide what deserves more investment, and explain why the direction needs to change.

The original idea may get a company started. The ability to respond to what the market is telling you can determine what it becomes.

Thank you, Kushal, for the honesty, and Abhinav, for bringing everyone together in Raipur.

Have you ever had to walk away from the version of your business you started with? I would like to hear what made you decide.

Save this for the next time your plan meets the market, and send it to a founder who is rethinking their direction.
