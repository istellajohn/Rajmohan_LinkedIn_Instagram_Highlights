## Caption

A question I keep coming back to with business families: what is your excess, and what is your deficit?

Most families carry both. Plenty of assets and very little liquidity. Several businesses and properties, and no single view of what is owned and what is owed.

The instinct is often to build a large family office to fix it. For many families, I don't think that is the answer. The goal is a lasting family legacy, not a bigger structure.

Slide 10 is a two-column audit you can take to your next family meeting. Which column would your family find harder to fill in honestly?
